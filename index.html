Connect Four solved, on every board size

Drop discs; four in a row wins. The numbers above the columns are a perfect player's verdict for the side to move: win in 5 means five more of your own discs, with best play on both sides. They come from a C solver compiled to WebAssembly and running in your tab. The same C file, compiled natively, recomputed John Tromp's table of who wins on each board size and his counts of legal positions — on the side.
